## About Me
For over 20 years, the Linux terminal has been my playground. I thrive on architecting **scalable infrastructure**, **automating workflows**, and delivering robust solutions that power innovation. My expertise spans **cloud platforms**, **infrastructure engineering**, and **platform orchestration**, but my passion for learning keeps me diving into cutting-edge technologies daily.

I’m driven to make systems more secure, efficient, and reliable, championing **best practices**, **DevSecOps**, and **observability** in every project. Outside of tech, I explore **blockchain**, ride my motorcycle, walk my dog, or cherish moments with my kids. A cold beer and lively chats with friends? Always a yes!
